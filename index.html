About

I'm an electrical engineering student at Stanford, currently working in the Arbabian Lab building a synchronized, single-computer ROS2 capture system that combines four Orbbec Femto Mega RGB-D cameras, a TI IWR6843ISK mmWave radar, and a Bosch SR250/STM32 UWB radar into one time-aligned rosbag pipeline for gait research. My work spans the full stack, from board-level firmware and network infrastructure to the synchronization architecture that keeps every sensor aligned to a shared clock.

I'm broadly interested in RF sensing, multimodal perception, and the systems-level work that makes sensor fusion reliable in practice. I'm currently looking for Bay Area EE-focused internships for Summer 2027.

Research Interests

  • RF & radar sensing — FMCW mmWave radar signal processing, UWB ranging, and RF as a privacy-preserving alternative to vision-based sensing.
  • Multimodal sensor fusion — time-synchronizing heterogeneous sensors (cameras, radar, UWB) into a single coherent capture pipeline.
  • Systems & infrastructure — ROS2 architecture, embedded firmware (STM32), and the networking/hardware layer sensing systems run on.
  • Gait & biomechanics — using synchronized sensor data to characterize normal and abnormal gait patterns for clinical and robotics applications.

Multimodal Data Collection and Waveform Optimization for Radar-Based Human Activity Monitoring

Arbabian Lab, Stanford University · Arun Tamura · Quarterly report

Summarizes a quarter of work onboarding to a multimodal radar sensing system and optimizing an FMCW waveform for healthcare-oriented human activity monitoring. Includes hands-on capture sessions at the Living Lab (sit-to-stand, freezing/shuffling gait, falls, and mixed-activity sequences) and a full redesign of the indoor radar waveform, evaluated against the prior outdoor-tuned waveform through corner reflector tests, sky tests, and micro-Doppler motion comparisons.

Projects

Multimodal RF + RGB-D Gait Capture Platform

ROS2, Python/C++ · TI IWR6843ISK, Orbbec Femto Mega, STM32 · Active

A single-computer ROS2 system synchronizing four Orbbec Femto Mega RGB-D cameras, a TI IWR6843ISK mmWave radar, and a Bosch SR250/STM32 UWB radar into one time-aligned rosbag recording pipeline. Built the capture-gate synchronization architecture, diagnosed cross-subsystem frame loss (network, CPU, disk), and configured the dual-subnet sensor network (PoE camera/UWB switch plus a direct USB-Ethernet mmWave link). This is the active continuation of the work behind my earlier paper, extended to full multimodal, multi-camera synchronization.

Autonomous Mechatronic Balancer

Python, Computer Vision, Servo Control · Mentor: Wei Cai · Jun 2025 – Sep 2025

A closed-loop control system combining a CMOS camera and servo actuator to stabilize a ping-pong ball on a pivoting lever arm in real time. Built a real-time Python computer vision/ML pipeline to track ball position frame-by-frame and minimize control loop latency, with hardware-in-the-loop testing and iterative calibration validated through weekly quantitative performance reports.
